About

I work on the production line of fiber-optic PLC Splitters: monitoring output, yield, rework, and defects, training new operators, and building small tools in Excel and Python to make reporting faster and more reliable.

These projects show how I approach data: define the business question first, clean and validate the data, use SQL and statistics only where they answer the question, and finish with a clear recommendation and an estimated impact.

How to read each project

  1. Problem: the business question.
  2. Data: source, size, and cleaning steps.
  3. Analysis: method, code, and findings.
  4. Recommendation: actions based on findings.
  5. Impact: formula with explicit assumptions.

Manufacturing projects use simulated or anonymized data because factory data is confidential. Impact numbers are labeled "estimated" or "simulated".
